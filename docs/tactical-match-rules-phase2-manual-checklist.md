# Tactical Match Rules Phase 2 Manual Checklist

- Confirm passes and through balls no longer allow clearly offside receivers for both the player and AI.
- Confirm a foul outside the box produces an automated free-kick restart.
- Confirm a foul inside the box produces an automated penalty restart.
- Confirm a ball leaving the sideline becomes a throw-in.
- Confirm a ball leaving the byline becomes a corner or goal kick based on the last touch.
- Confirm active players lose stamina across minutes and from actions, and late-match movement/rating feels weaker.
- Confirm a tactical injury can appear during live play, debuff the unit, and appear in the post-match flow.
- Confirm a yellow plus second yellow sends a player off, removes them from active play, and records the dismissal.
- Confirm a straight red sends a player off and records a one-match competition-local suspension.
- Confirm a suspended or unavailable player skips tactical match entry and the fixture auto-simulates as `did_not_play`.
- Confirm a player red card during live tactical play ends direct control and simulates the remainder of the match.
